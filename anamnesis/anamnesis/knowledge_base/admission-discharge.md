# Admission and Discharge Procedure

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Planned Admission

A planned admission begins at the admissions desk, where identity is confirmed against the medical record number and the consent forms are completed. The admitting nurse records baseline observations, allergies and current medication, and assigns a bed in the receiving ward.

## Emergency Admission

A patient arriving through the emergency department is admitted only after triage and a decision to admit by the responsible clinician. Bed allocation is coordinated by the bed management desk, which holds a live view of ward occupancy across the hospital.

## Discharge Criteria

Discharge requires a documented decision by the responsible consultant, a completed discharge summary, and confirmation that the patient has a safe destination and any required medication. The discharge summary is sent to the patient's primary care clinician within one working day.

## Discharge Timing

The discharge lounge opens at 08:00 and closes at 20:00. Patients whose transport is delayed may wait in the discharge lounge so that the bed can be released and cleaned for the next admission.

## Transfers Between Wards

An internal transfer requires a handover between the sending and receiving nurse, covering current observations, outstanding investigations, medication due and any infection control precautions. The bed management desk records the transfer so that occupancy figures stay accurate.
