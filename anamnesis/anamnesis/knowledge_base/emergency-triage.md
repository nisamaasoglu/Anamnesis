# Emergency Department Triage Standard

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Triage Categories

Meridian General uses a five-level triage scale. Level one is immediate resuscitation, level two is very urgent with a target assessment within ten minutes, level three is urgent within sixty minutes, level four is standard within two hours, and level five is non-urgent within four hours. Triage is performed by a registered nurse trained in the scale.

## Reassessment

A waiting patient is reassessed at an interval determined by their triage level, and immediately if their condition, observations or level of pain changes. Reassessment is documented in the emergency record.

## Escalation

Any clinical concern that cannot be resolved at the bedside is escalated to the emergency department consultant on duty. Departmental crowding beyond the declared threshold triggers the hospital escalation plan, which opens additional assessment capacity and alerts bed management.

## Information for Waiting Patients

Waiting patients and their relatives are given an estimate of the current waiting time for their triage level. Estimates are indicative only, because a higher-priority arrival always takes precedence over waiting order.

## Unaccompanied Minors and Vulnerable Adults

An unaccompanied patient under the age of eighteen is assigned a named nurse and the safeguarding lead is informed. The same applies to any adult identified as vulnerable during triage.
