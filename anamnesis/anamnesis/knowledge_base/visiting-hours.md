# Visiting Hours and Visitor Access Policy

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## General Visiting Hours

General inpatient wards at Meridian General are open to visitors from 10:00 to 20:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. A maximum of two visitors may be at the bedside at any one time. Visitors are asked to leave the ward during the 13:00 to 14:00 rest period so that patients can sleep and clinical rounds can be completed without interruption.

## Intensive Care Unit

The Intensive Care Unit operates restricted access. Visiting is permitted from 14:00 to 15:00 and again from 19:00 to 20:00. Only one visitor per patient is admitted at a time, and that visitor must be a registered next of kin recorded on the patient's admission form. Children under the age of twelve may not enter the Intensive Care Unit. The charge nurse may suspend visiting entirely during an emergency or during a bedside procedure.

## Maternity and Neonatal Units

Partners of admitted patients may visit the maternity ward at any hour. All other visitors are limited to 11:00 to 19:00. In the neonatal unit, only the two parents or legal guardians recorded at admission are permitted, and access is granted through the ward intercom.

## Paediatric Ward

One parent or guardian may remain with a paediatric patient overnight, and a folding bed is provided on request. Sibling visits are permitted between 15:00 and 18:00 provided the sibling has no symptoms of infection.

## Exceptions and Compassionate Access

Requests outside the published hours are decided by the duty nurse manager. Compassionate access for patients receiving end-of-life care is granted without time restriction and without a limit on the number of visitors, subject to the space available at the bedside.
