# Pharmacy and Laboratory Service Standards

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Laboratory Opening Hours

The central laboratory is staffed continuously for urgent work. Routine processing runs from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and from 08:00 to 14:00 on Saturdays. Samples for routine tests received after the cut-off are processed the next working morning.

## Turnaround Targets

An urgent full blood count or basic metabolic panel is reported within sixty minutes of the sample reaching the laboratory. Routine biochemistry and haematology are reported within twenty-four hours. Microbiology culture results depend on growth and are reported as they become available, with a preliminary report at twenty-four hours.

## Critical Result Notification

A result outside the critical range is telephoned to the requesting clinical area immediately and the call is documented, in addition to being released into the record system.

## Outpatient Pharmacy

The outpatient pharmacy is open from 08:30 to 18:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays. Outside those hours, discharge medication is supplied by the on-call pharmacy service through the ward.

## Result Access for Patients

Patients may view released results through the patient portal. Results flagged for clinical review are held until the responsible clinician has reviewed them, so that a result is never returned to a patient without context.
