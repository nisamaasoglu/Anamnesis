# Patient Rights, Records and Consent

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Right to Information

Every patient has the right to an explanation of their condition, the proposed investigations and treatment, the alternatives, and the expected outcome, given in language they understand and with an interpreter where needed.

## Consent

Consent is obtained before any procedure and may be withdrawn at any time. For a patient who lacks capacity, the decision is made in that patient's best interests following the hospital's capacity assessment procedure and involving those close to the patient.

## Access to Records

A patient may request a copy of their medical record in writing through the health records office. The hospital responds within one calendar month. Identity is verified before any record is released, and a request made on a patient's behalf requires written authorisation.

## Confidentiality

Clinical information is shared only with staff involved in that patient's care, and only to the extent needed. Staff do not confirm or deny the presence of a patient to a caller without the patient's recorded consent.

## Complaints and Feedback

Concerns are first raised with the ward manager or clinic coordinator, who attempts resolution the same day. A formal complaint is submitted to the patient experience office, which acknowledges within three working days and gives a target date for a full response.
