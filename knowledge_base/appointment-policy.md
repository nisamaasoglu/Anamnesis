# Outpatient Appointment Policy

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Booking Channels

Outpatient appointments may be booked through the patient portal, by telephone through the central appointment line, or in person at the outpatient reception desk. Referrals from primary care are placed directly into the referral queue and triaged by the receiving department within three working days.

## Arrival and Registration

Patients are asked to arrive fifteen minutes before the scheduled appointment time to complete registration. A patient who arrives more than fifteen minutes late may be asked to wait for the next free slot or to rebook, at the discretion of the clinic coordinator.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling

Cancellations must be made at least twenty-four hours before the appointment so the slot can be reallocated. A patient who fails to attend three consecutive appointments without notice is referred back to the referring clinician before any further booking is made.

## Interpreters and Accessibility

Interpreting services are arranged free of charge and must be requested at least forty-eight hours in advance. Wheelchair assistance, hearing loop rooms and step-free routes are available in every outpatient building; requests are recorded on the booking so they are ready on arrival.

## Appointment Reminders

An automated reminder is sent two days before the appointment and again on the morning of the appointment. Reminders are sent by SMS unless the patient has recorded a different preferred contact method.
