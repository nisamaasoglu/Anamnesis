# Infection Prevention and Control

*Meridian General Hospital is a fictional institution. This document is illustrative sample content written for a software demonstration and is not clinical guidance.*

## Hand Hygiene

Hand hygiene is performed before touching a patient, before a clean procedure, after exposure to body fluids, after touching a patient, and after touching the patient's surroundings. Alcohol-based hand rub is the default; soap and water are used when hands are visibly soiled.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Gloves and an apron are worn for any contact with body fluids. A fluid-resistant surgical mask and eye protection are added where splashing is anticipated. Protective equipment is removed and disposed of inside the patient's room, and hand hygiene is performed immediately afterwards.

## Isolation Precautions

Patients with a suspected or confirmed transmissible infection are placed in a single room with the door closed and a precaution sign displayed. Contact precautions apply to organisms spread by touch, droplet precautions to organisms spread by respiratory droplets, and airborne precautions require a negative-pressure room and a respirator.

## Visitor Restrictions During an Outbreak

During a declared outbreak the infection control team may reduce visiting to one named visitor per patient, suspend visiting on the affected ward, or require visitors to wear protective equipment. Restrictions are reviewed every forty-eight hours.

## Cleaning and Decontamination

A vacated bed space is cleaned before reuse. Rooms that held a patient under isolation precautions receive an enhanced clean, including curtain change, and are released by the ward manager before the next admission.
